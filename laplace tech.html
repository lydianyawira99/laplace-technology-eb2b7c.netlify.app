Frequently asked questions

Where are you located, and which areas do you serve?

We are based in Kikuyu Town, Kiambu County. Share your site location when you enquire and we'll confirm availability for your area.

How do I get a quote for a solar system?

Use the solar estimator above and send your estimate, or book a site survey. We give a detailed quote once we understand your loads and site.

Can you work on an existing system someone else installed?

Yes. We can inspect, troubleshoot, upgrade or maintain existing electrical and solar systems.

Do you only sell hardware, or do you install it too?

Both. You can buy hardware only, or have us supply, install and commission it for you.

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes. Routine maintenance keeps systems safe and efficient. Contact us to agree a schedule that suits your site.

How are online orders and bookings confirmed?

Your order or booking arrives on our WhatsApp or email. We reply to confirm price, availability, date and payment before anything is final.
